1. Executive Summary & Panel Critique Alignment

The Thesis Defense Panel's Core Finding:
"The web dashboard is overwhelming and cluttered. Too many raw metrics, multi-sensor Z-score deviations, isolation forest outlier bars, feature importance distributions, and regression evaluation tables compete for visual attention simultaneously on a single viewport."

An urban air quality monitoring system serves two distinct user classes with differing cognitive needs:

  1. Community Stakeholders (Residents, Barangay Officials, Students, Vendors): Evaluated using the IBM Computer Usability Satisfaction Questionnaire (CSUQ). They need immediate, non-technical clarity: "Is the air safe right now?" and "What will the air quality be in 1 to 3 hours?"
  2. Technical Experts & Defense Panelists (IT Developers, System Admins, Environmental Scientists): Evaluated using the ISO/IEC 25010 Software Quality Standard. They require transparent verification of underlying algorithms ($R^2 = 0.952$, MAE comparison, Isolation Forest anomaly attribution, and multi-sensor telemetry).

The Solution: Rather than stripping away features, this specification applies Progressive Disclosure. We decouple the interface into a primary, calming Citizen Overview layer and an on-demand Technical & AI Diagnostics layer.

4. Ergonomics, Spacing & Design System Tokens

Primary Dark BG #0B0F17 (Deep Slate)
Card Surface (Dark) #141C2B (Elevated Navy)
Primary Light BG #F8FAFC (Warm Neutral)
Accent Color #00CFA8 (Mint Emerald)
  • Concentric Border Radii: Outer card containers use border-radius: 16px; inner badges, progress bars, and pills use border-radius: 8px to create optical harmony.
  • Tabular Numerals: Dynamic sensor readings apply font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so numbers update every few seconds without layout twitching.
  • Breathable Whitespace: Card inner padding is elevated to 20px–24px with a grid gap of 16px, preventing the cramped visual density of the previous layout.
  • Accessible Hit Targets: All buttons, modal triggers, and navigation elements have at least 44px × 44px touch areas.

6. Panel Defense Presentation & Justification Script

When presenting this redesigned interface to your thesis panel, use these structured talking points to explain how their feedback was systematically resolved:

Opening Statement for System Demo:
"Honorable members of the panel, during our previous review, you provided a critical insight regarding cognitive load—specifically that our initial interface presented too much raw data and technical diagnostics all at once. In response, we conducted a rigorous UX re-architecting process based on the ISO/IEC 25010 Usability standard and the IBM Computer Usability Satisfaction Questionnaire (CSUQ)."
Key Defense Talking Points:
  • Application of Progressive Disclosure: Rather than stripping away features or dumbing down our capstone deliverables, we implemented the industry-standard principle of Progressive Disclosure. The interface now features a two-tiered hierarchy.
  • Solving the Problem for General Citizens: Everyday barangay stakeholders first experience the Citizen Overview. In less than 3 seconds, they see clear, color-coded air safety guidance, RA 8749 health advisories, and an immediate 3-hour predictive outlook without being overwhelmed by mathematical equations or sensor drift parameters.
  • Full Retention for Technical Evaluators: For technical experts and the defense panel, our Technical & AI Diagnostics Center is accessible with a single click. It houses 100% of our academic benchmarks—including our Random Forest $R^2 = 0.952$ score, the 81.2% MAE error reduction over Linear Regression, our Isolation Forest anomaly risk score, and our multi-sensor Z-score attribution grids.
  • Empirical Evaluation Readiness: This architecture allows both our non-technical respondents (via CSUQ) and technical experts (via ISO 25010) to rate the system with top scores without conflicting usability requirements.
